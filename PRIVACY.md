# Privacy Policy

Dalex: Chrome Cleaner does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal data or browsing data.

- No user data is collected or shared.
- All operations are performed locally in your browser.
- No analytics, no tracking, no telemetry.
- Cleaning preferences (data type toggles and default time range) are stored using Chrome Sync storage. If Chrome Sync is enabled, Chrome may synchronize those preferences through your Google account. The extension developer does not receive them.
- Chrome may synchronize history deletion across your devices if history sync is enabled.
